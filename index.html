iLanis App Factory · idea #9

Work when it's green.
Rest when it's red.

Pulse runs your intervals so you don't have to count. Set work, rest, and rounds - or tap a Tabata preset - and the whole screen tells you what to do, with audio cues you can hear over music.

Start a workout
⏰

Any interval

Work from 5 seconds to 5 minutes, rest the same, up to 40 rounds. Total time is shown before you start.

🔳

Tabata presets

Classic Tabata 20/10 x8, a 40/20 builder, and even 30/30 - one tap and go.

🔊

Audio cues

A countdown beep in the last three seconds of every phase and a chime when you switch. Eyes optional.

💡

Color-coded screen

Green means work, red means rest. Readable from across the room, mid-burpee.

No sign-up. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser.
